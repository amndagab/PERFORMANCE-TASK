PROJECT LOVE

An Original Multimodal One-Act Play

CHARACTERS

  • LEON (40s): A widowed former deep-space astronaut that retired to run a diner. Worn, protective, and driven by love and survival.
  • CHARLIE (14 at start / 30 & 46 in transmissions): Leon’s daughter. Emotionally intuitive, stubborn, deeply attached to her father.
  • THE FIGURE / ETERNAL CHARLIE: A multi-dimensional manifestation within the singularity. Speaks with calm, cosmic omniscience.
  • DR. VANCE (Voiceover / Stage Presence): Lead physicist of the Global Space Initiative. Cold and practical.

Project LOVE One-Act

SETTING:Planet Silvanus where Leon’s mission takes place to save humanity and allow humans to repopulate on this planet instead of the dying Earth.

[Leon on the distant planet Silvanus, where life thrives underneath the dark abyss of a black hole.]

[Screen pops up and says (Recording message) with a female voice saying what is on the screen.]

LEON: It's undeniable that love is the force that allows us to comprehend the mysteries of time and space.

[STAGE DIRECTION: Leon then walks into the distance, looking at his laboratory.]

LEON: It's only been 10 minutes, and I have missed that blue speck in the sky. For it is okay, since they have given me the tools to repopulate the human race. Cells of fertility are present and are in the process of incubation.

[The AI watch assistant alerts Leon with a loud beeping sound, accompanied with a voice saying]

Detecting extreme time dilation, estimating time on earth in the planet Sylvanus. 1 second passed is 10 minutes relative to Earth.

[As a faint tick of a clock echoes through the air for each second passed]

LEON: Time is really important, and it is all I need to nourish the little time I have.

LEON: I have given the tools to repopulate the human race, I have recordings of everything they will need to nurture and to prosper.

[Leon looks at the distant black hole, without a single thought in his mind.]

LEON: Today has taken up my energy. I should take a nap.

[STAGE DIRECTION: He walks into his tiny bedroom, tucking himself into bed and falling into a deep sleep.]

[Through a dream, a flashback of his past replays. Back on earth, back when the future he is in was just a decision.]

DR. VANCE: The power source is at 99.999994%. You will reach Silvanus in just 4 days, Leon. But due to extreme time dilation... 30 years will pass on Earth before you even land. Are you up for this? [Charlie, now understanding the math, freezes. A few seconds pass by as they wait for Leon to decide.]

LEON:(Taking a deep breath, he whispers to himself as he looks at the deep luminous sky) Am I really saving humanity if it comes at the cost of losing the person I love most? [Facing a moral dilemma despite knowing he does not have any other choice.]

[STAGE DIRECTION: Suddenly, the overhead fluorescent lights on stage begin to rhythmically flicker in an erratic sequence.]

LIGHT PULSE SEQUENCING (MORSE CODE):

-.. --- -. - / .-.. . .- ...-

[ D O N T / L E A V E ]

CHARLIE: (Gasping, pointing at the flashing lights) Look! The lights! `-.. --- -. - / .-.. . .- ...- .`... It says 'DON'T LEAVE'! Someone... something is telling me to stop you! Dad, don't leave! Please!

LEON: (Grabbing his helmet, tearing up) Charlie, listen to me! It's okay. When I come back to Earth, you’ll be the same age as me, and we’ll celebrate your birthday together! I’m sorry, I need to save humanity, Charlie.

CHARLIE: You keep saying you need to save humanity! But what humanity are you saving if you have to abandon the people you love?! What humanity are you saving if you have to abandon ME?!

LEON: (Speechless, but set in stone.) I’m sorry, I love you, Charlie.

CHARLIE: (Screaming, weeping, kicking and biting as guards restrain her) NO! DONT LEAVE ME! I HAVE NO ONE, DAD! DONT LEAVE ME!

LEON: (Being pulled into the airlock, crying heavily) DON'T WORRY! I'LL BE BACK!

CHARLIE: (Voice ringing out in agony) SOMEONE IS TELLING ME NOT TO!

[The airlock seals shut. The engines detonate with blinding white light.]

[Leon jolts awake. It has been years since he last saw his daughter, ever since that day.]

[STAGE DIRECTION: He spots the terrifying, swirling event horizon of a supermassive black hole in the corner of his eye. As he turns to look at it, the darkness glints off a picture of his beloved Charlie. Without thought, he runs towards it and attempts to hug the reflection, ending up in an endless void of nothingness.]

[STAGE DIRECTION: Sound turns into deafening gravitational groans. The lights warp into extreme optical distortions. The ship fractures. Spaghettification effects stretch the visual space. Then... utter, total pitch-black silence.]

[Leon closes his eyes, bracing for death.]

[A warm, golden light slowly shows across the stage, replacing the black void. A luminous figure, “THE FIGURE” stands before him. The figure embodies all forms at once, radiating intense warmth.]

LEON: (Opening his eyes, shielding his face) ...Am I dead?

THE FIGURE: Have you been taking care of me?

LEON: Who... who are you?

THE FIGURE: I am you, and you are the entirety of the universe.

LEON: What does that mean?

THE FIGURE: You are beautiful. You have been taking care of me.

LEON: (His voice catching in his throat) Did... did you tell my daughter that message? The lights... the Morse code?

THE FIGURE: Yes. I did.

LEON: (Desperate) How is she now?! Is she okay?!

THE FIGURE: Do not worry, for all of you humans are yourselves... but if you ask me, she died peacefully.

LEON: What is your name?

THE FIGURE: I am what remained, I am what shall come.

LEON: What.. are you?

THE FIGURE: I am who we were. I am who we become. I am everything you refused to forget.

[The Figure steps closer, its voice shifting slightly, echoing with the soft, tender, unmistakable cadence of his daughter.]

THE FIGURE: Hahaha! You're so funny Dada! [The voice of a memory from when Charlie was a toddler.] Woah! That’s so cool Daddy! [The voice of a memory from when Charlie was a kid.] I love your chili, Dad. Could you teach me how to make it? [The voice of a memory from when Charlie was a pre-teen.] I graduated, Dad. I miss you. [The voice of an adult Charlie.] My kids love your recipes, dad. I wish they could have met who made them. [The voice of Charlie as a mother.]

[A deafening silence surrounded the abyss for a few moments. Suddenly, the voice of a sickly old woman resonates in space. Charlie on her deathbed.] I love you, don't cry. You haven't left my heart, dad.

[Leon cries—not out of grief, but out of guilt.]

THE FIGURE: Leon, you chose this fate, ignored the warnings.. But it doesn’t mean you made a mistake. Everything you taught her, it lived, even in your absence. What you loved was not meaningless just because it ended.

LEON: (His tears uncontrollably rolling down his face.) I left her again. I failed her. I failed my girl, again.

THE FIGURE: [Speaking in a subdued, gentle tone.] Leon, leaving isn’t always abandonment. Sometimes, leaving is an act of love when what you leave behind allows others to continue. And sometimes, staying isn’t the only option when it comes to loving someone.

[Leon listens and reflects, he kneels down as the warm golden light wraps around him like a blanket. His eyes slowly close into a deep, dreamless sleep, floating in the cosmic abyss.]

[ACT END]

Author's Note

Project LOVE does not just tell a story of space travel and time dilation. Rather, it is a reflection of the world we live in.

In today's reality, citizens are compelled to leave their homes— not out of desire, but out of necessity, and out of their love for the people they provide for. Represented through Leon's journey of decision when faced with a system larger than himself and the mission to save humanity, overseas workers live far from their loved ones to provide better futures. They are seperated from their families, sometimes, without knowing the underlying danger that comes from the country they choose to reside in, such as Leon's impulsive decision of entering the black hole. Furthermore, communities are challenged by conflict, climate disasters, or economic hardship. Like Leon, people leave not to abandon, but as an act of love for their loved ones.

Charlie embodies the voices that are left behind. She is the reflection of today's youth— forced to grow up quickly in a world shaped by decisions they did not make. Charlie becomes a symbol of memory, culture, and truth through her video messages. Her messages reminds us that what truly sustains humanity is not defined by technology and advancement, rather, the preservation of identity, relationships, and shared experiences.

The deteriorating Earth does not just mirror the ongoing crisis of climate change and environmental collapse, but also the ideas of social inequality and moral fatigue. The desperation of humanity to uphold survival for a few over dignity for all is enough to prove that we tend to prioritize escape rather than repair. Project LOVE challenges this idea: Have we truly survived if we carry our bodies into the future but leave behind the values that make us human?

The figure serves as a reminder that what unites us is far wider than what seperates us in a society where differences create divisions. It represents collective human experience, that identity is not individual, but it is shared— not confined to one lifetime, but echoed through generations.

In life, we are challenged to make decisions against our will. To choose between what is right and what is necessary, between staying and leaving, between self and others. These decisions don't just define who we are as individuals, but also what kind of world we want to create. Ultimately, Project LOVE is about responsibility— it questions what it means to move forward, not just physically, but morally. Because in the end, humanity is not preserved by distance or technology, but by love, dignity, and the choices we dare to make.

-The Team
