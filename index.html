Editor's Note

It is truly mesmerizing how one task gives us an opportunity at exploring a number of fields. Through this website, we became playwrights, literary analysts, layout designers, reflection writers, and web developers— mistakes were inevitable, yet, to the best of our abilities, we took these roles to heart and fulfilled each and every responsibility step-by-step. At the end of the day, each paragraph, each line of code, and each page documents the efforts and lessons we exercised into crafting this website. From there, it becomes proof that teamwork and the drive to do the best is enough to defy our deepest doubts, and do what we thought we couldn't. After all, there is fulfillment in conquering what once scared us.

In this website, we take a deeper look at dignity and morality, and emphasize how something as natural as death does not erase human dignity. The deceased live on through the memories they left behind and the lessons they taught. Through the application of this central theme "Dignity Beyond Death" unto analyzing literature such as Dekada '70 and our very own one-act play titled Project Love, as well as reflecting off of it through our topics in CLVED— this site becomes a tribute for our loved ones who have passed, all victims of unjust deaths, and every soul that deserves to be recognized not as someone who is dead, but as someone who made the world a better place in their own way. The creation of this site made us realize that what we once loved was not meaningless just because it ended.

Through this site, the team wishes to share this perspective to the world.

— Group One, Your Number One
